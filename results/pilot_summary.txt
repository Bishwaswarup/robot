Suggested reading (decide on section 1, not on p-values):
  * STRONG: phase-gated falls in a good share of push/cmd conditions, or tilt /
    recovery swings >~30% of the mean -> gait phase belongs in the swarm layer.
  * WEAK:  no phase-gated falls and swings <~10-15% -> phase washes out; model
    feasibility on speed/turn state (+ slew limits) only.
  * in between: phase matters only near the limits -> keep it as a safety margin
    term (e.g. shrink the feasible set at bad phases), not a headline variable.
